# Learnova
AI-powered Android study app for creating notes, flashcards, and quizzes.
Learnova
A smarter way to learn.
Learnova is an AI-powered study app designed to help students learn more effectively.
Planned Features
AI Study Notes: Turn learning materials into clear, organised notes.
Flashcards: Create flashcards to remember important facts and definitions.
Quizzes: Test your knowledge with AI-generated questions.
Subject Organisation: Keep study materials organised by subject.
Photo Uploads: Turn photos of textbook pages and handwritten notes into study resources.
Manual Creation: Create and edit your own notes, flashcards, and quizzes.
Our Goal
Learnova aims to understand the meaning of study materials, identify important concepts, and help students understand what they learn rather than simply memorise words.
Platform
Android — with a modern, AI-powered design.
Learnova is currently in development
